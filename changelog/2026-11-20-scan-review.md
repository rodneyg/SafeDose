## Improved
- After a photo scan, the review screen lists the syringe and vial details SafeDose detected alongside your photo.
- Check those details against your syringe and vial label. Read the syringe fill line yourself, then follow the on-screen prompt to confirm or enter details manually. You can retake the photo from the same screen.
